# micropython-pydevices and the device modules

The PyDevices patch queue for MicroPython: an ordered series of patches over a pinned upstream release, plus presets, boards and variants that build the PyDevices C modules into firmware with upstream's own `make`. The device modules `displayif`, `audioif`, `cameraif` and `usbhost` are built here, in its `modules/` folder, and reach you in the firmware.

[micropython-pydevices and the device modules on GitHub](https://github.com/PyDevices/micropython-pydevices).
