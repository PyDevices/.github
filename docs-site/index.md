OmniPython is the portability layer for MicroPython, CircuitPython and
CPython: the libraries your program imports to draw, play sound, take touch
and talk USB, the same on a board, a desktop, a phone and a web page. This
site is the manual for every one of them. The sidebar lists them, and search
looks through all of them at once.

If you are new, start with the [newcomer's guide](newcomers.md). To get it on
your machine or your board, [install](install.md) it.
